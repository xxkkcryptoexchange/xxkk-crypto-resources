04 / Risk

Security and Risk Awareness

Crypto markets can be volatile, and product-specific risks can differ significantly. A careful review of the current product documentation is an important step before using any exchange or trading service.

01Market risk

Prices can change rapidly, and losses can occur when an asset moves against a position.

02Leverage risk

Leverage increases exposure relative to margin and can magnify both gains and losses.

03Liquidation risk

Leveraged positions may be closed when account equity or margin falls below required levels.

04Fees & terms

Check trading fees, funding, conversion rates, withdrawal limits, and current product terms.

05Account security

Use strong security practices and verify domains, messages, devices, and withdrawal details.

05 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is XXKK?

XXKK is a cryptocurrency platform with publicly described products that include spot trading, perpetual contracts, copy trading, asset conversion, and asset management.

What services does XXKK offer?

Public product information describes spot trading, perpetual contracts, copy trading, asset conversion, and asset management. Availability and terms can vary, so confirm the current offering on the official platform.

What is spot trading?

Spot trading involves buying or selling an asset in a spot market. Unlike a derivative position, the transaction is generally based on the underlying digital asset itself.

What are perpetual contracts?

Perpetual contracts are derivatives that generally do not have a fixed expiration date. They can involve margin, funding payments, leverage, and liquidation risk.

What is crypto copy trading?

Copy trading is a feature that lets users mirror selected trading activity from another trader. It does not guarantee the same outcome and can still result in losses.

What is asset conversion?

Asset conversion is the process of exchanging one supported digital asset for another using a quoted rate. Users should review the rate, fees, limits, and confirmation details.

What should users consider before using a crypto exchange?

Review supported assets, fees, product mechanics, security controls, withdrawal conditions, account requirements, terms, and any rules that apply to your location.

Is cryptocurrency trading risky?

Yes. Crypto prices can be highly volatile, while derivatives can add leverage, margin, funding, and liquidation risks. No trading outcome is guaranteed.
